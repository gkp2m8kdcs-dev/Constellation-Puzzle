Frequently asked

How do I play?

Open an arena, tap stars to start a chain. Tap the first star again, or hit Forge, to close the polygon. Three stars is the smallest constellation; larger ones pay more. Every forge is logged in your gallery.

The arena seems off — my stars don't connect. What gives?

Constellation Puzzle draws straight lines between the stars you've tapped. If two stars lie on top of each other or the chain overlaps itself, the polygon may not be valid. Clear the chain and try a different starting star.

How do I change my pilot name or avatar?

From the home screen, tap your pilot card on the top-right. You'll be taken to Profile → Edit, where you can change your name, pick a new avatar from your photo library, or capture a new one with the camera.

Why is my Daily Forge streak not counting?

The streak counts a Day if you forge at least one constellation in the Daily Forge before your local midnight. If your phone's clock is set to the wrong time or timezone, the streak may appear to skip a day.

Can I move my gallery to a new iPhone?

Constellation Puzzle does not sync to any cloud, so gallery data does not move automatically. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a Mac, the app's local data is included in that backup and can be restored on a new device.

Does the app show ads, collect data, or make you sign in?

No. Constellation Puzzle has no accounts, no analytics SDK, no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no push notifications. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

I found a bug / I'd like a feature.

Please email Mefepzavap@icloud.com with a short description. If it's a bug, including your iPhone model and iOS version helps a lot.

How do I request a refund?

Constellation Puzzle is sold as a one-time purchase with no in-app purchases. Refund requests are handled directly by Apple — visit reportaproblem.apple.com or contact Apple Support.
