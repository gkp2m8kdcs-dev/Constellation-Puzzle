Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026 · Effective immediately.

Short version. Constellation Puzzle does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. There is no account system, no analytics SDK, no advertising network, no remote leaderboard, no push notification, and no in-app purchase. Everything the app saves — your pilot profile, your avatar photo, your gallery, your achievements, your daily streak — is stored locally on your iPhone. You can delete all of it in two taps from Settings.

1. Who we are

Constellation Puzzle (also referenced internally as "StellarForge") is a single-player iOS application. For any privacy question you can reach the developer at Mefepzavap@icloud.com.

2. Data we do not collect

Constellation Puzzle does not, and will not, collect any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, account ID).
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, advertising IDs, MAC addresses).
  • Location data (precise or coarse).
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, performance telemetry.
  • Diagnostic or advertising data of any kind.
  • In-app purchase history — there is no purchase system.
  • Push notification tokens — there is no push system.

3. Data that lives on your device

The app stores the following data locally on your iPhone. None of it is uploaded anywhere.

3.1 Pilot profile

  • Pilot name — the display name you type in (optional, free text).
  • Avatar photo — the photo you pick from your library or capture with the camera (optional).
  • Storage location: the app's Documents directory, inside the app sandbox.

3.2 Gameplay state

  • Arena progress, game mode preferences, gallery entries, and per-arena personal bests.
  • Daily Forge streak counter and last-played timestamp.
  • Achievement unlock flags.
  • Storage location: UserDefaults and the app's Documents directory.

3.3 System permissions the app may request

Constellation Puzzle only asks for the permissions it strictly needs, and only when you take the action that triggers them.

  • Photos (read) — to let you pick an avatar from your library. The chosen image is stored locally; the app does not upload it.
  • Camera — to let you capture an avatar in-app. The capture is stored locally; the app does not upload it.
  • Photos (add) — to let you save a constellation snapshot back to your photo library. Triggered only when you tap the save action.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time from your iPhone's Settings app, without affecting local gameplay.

4. No third parties

Constellation Puzzle does not integrate analytics, advertising, attribution, crash reporting, A/B testing, or social login SDKs. There are no cookies, beacons, or tracking pixels — the app has no web view. The app makes no outbound network request related to your identity or gameplay.

5. Children

The app does not target children and does not knowingly collect any data from any user, regardless of age. Because no data leaves the device, there is no special handling needed for minors.

6. Your rights and controls

  • Inspect. All local data is accessible inside the app (Profile, Gallery, Achievements, Streak).
  • Edit. You can change your pilot name and avatar at any time from the Profile screen.
  • Delete. Settings → "Reset local data" wipes profile, gallery, achievements, and streak. You can also delete the app to remove everything from your device.
  • Export. If you ever need a copy of your gallery, contact us and we will guide you through exporting it from inside the app.

7. International transfers

Because Constellation Puzzle does not transmit personal data to any server, there are no international data transfers to disclose. All processing happens on your iPhone.

8. Security

Data is stored in the standard iOS app sandbox, which is protected by iOS itself. Constellation Puzzle does not introduce additional network connections, so there is no network-side attack surface for personal data.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the change will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Because Constellation Puzzle does not collect contact information, the developer will not proactively notify users of changes. Material changes — for example, the introduction of any analytics SDK — will be announced prominently inside the app before they take effect.

10. Contact

Questions, complaints, or data requests: Mefepzavap@icloud.com. We respond personally — there is no automated triage, and no third party handling your message.
