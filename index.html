Forge constellations.

Constellation Puzzle is a quiet, single-player geometry puzzle for the fifteen minutes before bed. Chain stars into polygons, log them in your gallery, and keep a personal streak going. Everything stays on your iPhone — no login, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases.

Forge. Log. Repeat.

A puzzle that respects your time.

No login walls. No energy timers. No “come back tomorrow” nudges. Just stars, your finger, and an arena.

01

Chain by tap

Tap stars to start a chain. Tap the first star again — or hit Forge — to close the polygon. Three stars is the minimum. Larger polygons pay more.

02

Log every constellation

Each forge is captured with a generated name, the arena it came from, and the score it earned. Your gallery grows quietly over time.

03

Pilot profile

Set a pilot name and a hex avatar from your photo library. The card is yours — wipe it from Settings in two taps, no questions asked.

Three modes. Pick a mood.

Constellation Puzzle gives you a different shape of session depending on how much time you have.

Classic · 12 rounds

Classic Forge

Twelve constellations per arena, end to end. A measured pace for the kind of evening where you actually want to finish something.

Time attack · 90 seconds

Time Attack

Ninety seconds. Every arena keeps its own personal best. Tight, repeatable, easy to retry on the bus.

Endless

Endless Drift

The arena stays open until you stop. No counter, no countdown — just stars and your rhythm.

Daily · shared seed

Daily Forge

One fixed constellation per day, rotating across all six arenas. Every pilot on the planet sees the same stars. Your streak keeps count.

Six arenas, hand-built.

Each arena is its own visual world — different palette, different density, different personality.

Atrium Foundry

An iron workshop. The first arena. Slow stars, warm light.

Gothic Cathedral

Stained-glass panes frame the night sky. Quiet, devotional geometry.

Skateyard Nebula

Lemon-gold rails, scattered decks. Bright and restless.

Meteor Hollow

Ember-lit, low gravity. Larger polygons come easier here.

Great Forge

For veteran pilots. Dense fields and longer chains.

Deep Vault

The silent arena. Sparse stars, deep blacks, no music.
